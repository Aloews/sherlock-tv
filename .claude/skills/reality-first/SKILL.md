---
name: reality-first
description: Evidence protocol for catalog, media playback, integrations and delivery.
---
Inspect actual repository, runtime, browser and deployment configuration. Establish exact HEAD, branch, dirty state, source catalog, relay contract, player implementation and baseline. Classify claims as VERIFIED, CORROBORATED, ESTIMATE, ASSUMPTION, UNKNOWN or DISPUTED. Distinguish catalog response, selected manifest, media segments and actual playback. Record browser/network evidence with secrets redacted. Never claim channel health or playback without a real player test.
