# Sherlock — product concept

## Product

Sherlock is a persistent, systemic simulation of human civilization. It models how material conditions, people, knowledge, organizations, institutions, power and culture interact over time. The world produces its own history through rules, constraints, decisions, incomplete information and shocks; it is not a scripted sequence of historical events.

The simulation is the primary product. Football, sports organizations and real-world football data are optional specialized domains that participate in the same economy and institutions. They must not dictate the architecture.

## Connected systems

1. **Planet and ecology:** geography, climate, water, soil, minerals, energy, biodiversity and natural hazards.
2. **People and settlements:** individuals, households, skills, health, education, birth, death, migration, cities and social groups.
3. **Knowledge and technology:** discovery, research, diffusion, education and infrastructure.
4. **Production and exchange:** land, labor, capital, firms, goods, services, supply chains, logistics, trade and prices.
5. **Money and finance:** accounting, currencies, credit, debt, collateral, interest, banks, payments, investment, liquidity, defaults and crises.
6. **Organizations and institutions:** firms, guilds, banks, universities, cultural bodies, courts, states and regulators.
7. **Power and politics:** authority, legitimacy, coalitions, taxation, public goods, conflict, diplomacy and institutional change.
8. **Dynasties and intergenerational capital:** families, inheritance, alliances, ownership, reputation, education and succession.
9. **Culture and shared belief:** language, identity, religion, myths, status, trust, norms, media and expectations.
10. **Historical memory:** archives, records, education, institutional learning and reinterpretation.
11. **Specialized domains:** football and other industries built on shared population, labor, capital, infrastructure, finance and culture.

## Player and observer

The player may act as an individual, household, organization, dynasty, state or independent observer depending on the scenario. The role is scenario configuration, not a hard-coded assumption in the world kernel. Actions require resources, access and information, and can create delayed or unintended consequences.

## Time and knowledge

The simulation supports pause, single-step and fast-forward. Different systems may operate at different frequencies. Agents act on information they possess or believe, not omniscient world state.

## Evidence model

Historical source records, scholarly interpretations, model assumptions, derived estimates and generated simulation outcomes remain distinct. Imported facts require provenance and uncertainty where available. Conflicting sources remain representable. Generated events are never presented as verified history or current real-world data.

## Non-negotiable properties

- One connected world, not isolated minigame economies.
- Causal, inspectable events with actor, cause, time, affected entities and provenance.
- Generational continuity for people, households, firms, banks, states and institutions.
- Deterministic replay for a fixed seed, ruleset and input sequence.
- Explicit units, time scales, ownership and accounting contracts.
- No silent conversion of estimates or assumptions into facts.
- Specialized domains integrate through shared domain contracts.
